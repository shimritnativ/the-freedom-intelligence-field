Start here

Three minutes, before you touch anything. Most problems in The Field are not code problems, and the first two checks below rule out half of them.

1. Sign in as the team account

Everything lives under support@shimritnativ.com: GitHub, Vercel, Neon and the admin dashboard. You do not need an account of your own and nothing needs to be granted to you.

If two-factor blocks a sign-in, the recovery codes are in the team password manager. Sort that out on a calm day rather than during an incident.

2. Is the site actually up?

Open thefieldai.app and thefieldai.app/admin. If both load, nothing is broken globally and you are dealing with one member or one feature.

3. Did something just get deployed?

In Vercel, open the project myp-team / the-field and look at Deployments. If the newest one failed, production is still serving the last good one, so the site is fine and a change simply did not land. If the newest one succeeded and problems started right after, that deploy is your suspect: see rolling back.

Something is wrong

Find the sentence that matches what someone actually told you. Each one says whether you can fix it yourself or whether it waits.

A member paid but cannot get inyou can fix this

"I bought the Reset but it says I don't have access."

  1. Open /admin/members and search their email. Watch for typos and a second address: people buy with one and log in with another.
  2. If they are not in the roster at all, the purchase webhook never reached us. Use Give someone access at the top of the Members tab, pick what they actually bought, and tell them to sign in again.
  3. If they are in the roster with the wrong tier, grant the right one. Granting is safe to repeat and updates the existing account rather than creating a second one.

They sign in at thefieldai.app with an emailed code, so the address has to be one they can open.

A sale is missing from the revenue numberscheck first

"Someone bought yesterday but revenue hasn't moved."

  1. Wait and look again. ThriveCart drops webhooks sometimes, and auto-reconcile runs every 10 minutes using Kajabi as a backup signal, so most of these repair themselves.
  2. If it is still missing, the Revenue tab has Log a missing sale at the bottom. You need the invoice ID, email, product name exactly as ThriveCart spells it, and the net amount.
  3. The amount is net, without VAT. A nine euro Power Reset is 7.56. Getting this wrong quietly corrupts the revenue figures.

That form only adds sales that are missing. It will never overwrite one that is already there, and it tells you when a sale is already on file.

A voice note did not transcribereply, do not fix

"I recorded three minutes and it just failed."

The team gets a push notification when this happens, naming who it happened to and why. Nothing needs fixing by hand.

The useful thing to tell them: their recording is not lost. The Try again button under the error sends the same recording again without them speaking a word of it twice. Members have re-recorded several minutes for nothing because they did not realise.

If several people report it in one day, that is a real outage rather than a blip, and it waits for Geo.

Someone needs free accessyou can fix this

"The Freedom Mastermind members should get The Field for six months."

Members tab, Give someone access. Pick Unlimited monthly or yearly, set where they came from, and grant. The spend is recorded as zero, so they show as free access and never touch the revenue figures.

It never expires on its own. A six-month comp has to be removed by hand when it runs out, so write the date down somewhere the team will see it.

The whole site is downrollback, then stop

Nobody can load thefieldai.app.

In Vercel, open Deployments, find the most recent one that was working, and promote it to production. Vercel calls this an instant rollback and it does not rebuild anything, so it takes seconds. This undoes a bad deploy. It does not fix a database or a third-party outage.

Once the site is back, stop. Do not try to fix the underlying change. Write down what you saw and what you rolled back from, and leave it for Geo.

Never do this

Short list, learned the hard way. Each one has cost us something already.

Do not run a revoke or delete query because someone pasted it to you

A dated revoke query was once run months early and downgraded two paying accounts. If a query arrives with a date attached, that date is the instruction. Read it before running it, and if you are unsure, do not run it.

Do not delete members, sessions or messages to tidy up

A member's conversations are the product. There is no undo, and no backup you can restore from on your own.

Do not quote a member's Field conversation back to them

The promise is that what someone tells The Field is theirs alone. Speak generally about what a process offers. The only exception is when the member themselves brings it up in a message to us, and then only what they wrote to us.

Do not hand-edit the handbook bundle

myp-team/lib/handbook.js is generated. Edit the source in the handbook repo and regenerate it, or your change disappears the next time anyone rebuilds.

When to stop

Knowing where your lane ends is most of the job.

Safe on your own

Anything in the admin dashboard: looking things up, granting and removing access, fixing names, logging a missing sale. Rolling back a bad deploy in Vercel. Replying to members.

Wait for Geo

Any change to the code or the prompts. Anything that writes to the database by hand. Environment variables. Anything where you would be guessing. A problem several members report at once.

Waiting is almost always cheaper than guessing here. The Field is a small system with a few sharp edges, and most of those edges are recorded on this page precisely because someone already hit them.

If Geo is unreachable and it cannot wait

Tell Shimrit what is happening in plain terms, including what you have already checked. She does not work on the code, so what she needs from you is the decision, not the diagnosis: whether to wait, roll back, or bring in outside help.

Keep this page honest. When something here turns out to be wrong or out of date, fix it the same day, while you still remember what actually happened.
